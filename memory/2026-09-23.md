## The findings worth keeping

**Swing–twist, not Euler**: Euler's sensitivity 609.9 at an arm raised forward (1/cos(pitch) = 573),
swing–twist 1.55. **The twist row is the half-way axis over cos(φ/2)**, derived from the swing as two
mirrors; a row about the bone is wrong by `|ω⊥|tan(φ/2)` and round a loop by the solid angle —
**Codman's paradox**, 90.000° for the octant, 48.231/180/360/540 for circles, to three decimals.

**The handoff is one assignment**: the chord a steered body carries IS semi-implicit Euler's
velocity; the Rodrigues vector inverts the linearised spin (1.57e-07 against the log's 9.75e-05 =
θ³/12). Momentum kept exactly, 204.40 kg·m/s; handed over at rest the character stops dead.

**7.7's walk bends the knees forward** (48° past the stop) and twists forearms off their hinges.
Nobody saw it for a module because a sampler does not care; a ragdoll's joints refuse it.

## The two bugs, and what found them

**8.11's position pass** solved satisfied one-sided rows against a zero bias, so a two-ended limit's
far stop cancelled the near one's correction. Found by a *probe*: printing a lying ragdoll's joint
angles against their limits (knee 9° hyperextended and creeping). Isolated: a loaded knee frozen at
−0.1691° for ten seconds. **8.10's islands** never let a kinematic body wake anything; found when a
steered character ran through a sleeping crate that moved fine with sleeping off.

## Five refusals (the 13th to 17th in Module 8)

The handover's forearm/torso overlap at rest (none within 4 cm; they touch in *falls*); the bone twist
row walking through its stop (it lags by drift/β, 9.08° vs 9.55° predicted); "each doubling of
sweeps halves the stretch" (a hanging body: 4.13 → 2.52 mm at 32 sweeps, 0.001 with sub-steps); the
deepest instant as a self-collision measure (it is the arrival depth); and "a settled ragdoll
sleeps" (4 of 12, at thresholds tuned on crates).
