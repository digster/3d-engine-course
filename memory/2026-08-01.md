## The two open decisions, settled

- **Depth buffer beside `framebuffer`, not inside it.** Decided by `SDL_BeginGPURenderPass`, which
  takes colour targets as an *array* and the depth-stencil target as a *separate, nullable*
  parameter. Also: colour-only consumers (2-D demos, Pong, HUD, Module 6 intermediates) would each
  pay 8 MB at 1080p for nothing.
- **The depth test lives in the rasterizer, not the buffer.** `SDL_GPUDepthStencilState` has
  `compare_op` + `enable_depth_test` + `enable_depth_write` as three independent pipeline knobs.
- **3.1 fills.** A wireframe writes almost no pixels; and with depth on and *no* culling, front
  faces beat back faces — making the point that culling (3.4) is an optimisation while the z-buffer
  is the correctness mechanism.

## The derivation (the lesson's spine)

Substitute the projection into a triangle's plane equation and `w` factors straight out:
`1/w = (1/d)[ (a·n_x/f)·x_n + (n_y/f)·y_n − n_z ]` — **1/w is affine in screen space**. Since
`z_ndc = −A + B·(1/w)`, device depth inherits that affinity *exactly*, so barycentric interpolation
of it is the exact answer, not an approximation. View z is `−1/(1/w)` — a hyperbola. That bracket
is also the entire tool 3.2 needs.

**The bug hides on flat walls**: if the plane is parallel to the screen, `1/w` is constant and both
choices agree — i.e. on exactly the boxes-and-floors geometry test scenes are made of.

## Verified, not asserted

- 199,273 random triangles: device depth wrong by ≤ `8.2e-13`; view z wrong by up to **5.73 units
  (243.6%)**.
- Worked example (`near=1, far=100`, `A=B=−100/99`): screen midpoint at `w=1.980198`; device-depth
  lerp gives **exactly** 0.5 (the truth); view-z lerp gives −50.5 against −1.98, **25.5×** off.
- Cycle planks at exactly `±0.55` per corner, basis determinant `+1.000000`. Disagreement:
  **144 px** (cycle), **162 px** (intersect).
- z-fight scene: **478/875 (54.6%)** wrong at D16, **0** at D24 and D32_FLOAT. Predicted: one D16
  code spans 0.00208 units at 6.4 units, gap 0.001 = 0.48 codes.
- Milestone scene occupies `[0.95032, 0.96200]` = **1.17%** of the depth range.
- Precision: `near` 1→0.1 costs **10.09×** everywhere; `far` 100→1000 costs **0.9%**.

## Two things the harness caught

- **The cycle artifact was 7 px** — correct geometry, invisible failure. A parameter sweep
  (circumradius, plank width, overhang) moved it to 144 px. *Showing the failure* requires the
  failure to be big enough to see, and that is a quantity you measure and tune.
- **The counter never read zero on the solids scene** (29 px). The silhouette-tie explanation was
  plausible; it was *tested* — cull back faces and it reads `6 of 12 tris kept, 0 px differ`. Now
  stated as fact in the pitfalls and handed to 3.4 by name.

## Caught by eyes, not by check-page

**Figure 2's near/far labels were inverted relative to its own geometry.** The eye sits at the
bottom of a top-down view, so the *lower* line is the nearer one — the labels and the pixel strip
both said the opposite. Found by reading the screenshot against the coordinates. `check-page.js`
cannot see this class of error and says so in its own header.

Also: widget SVGs are not `figure.dia` SVGs, so their `<text>` fell back to black and vanished in
dark mode (since 2.12). Fixed in the shared template; re-stamped 31 pages.
