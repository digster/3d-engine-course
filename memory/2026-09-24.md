## The findings worth keeping

**A cast is Newton on a convex distance, safe because each step stops at a separating plane** —
so it must step from GJK's lower bound. From the upper bound (`distance`): 377 of 6,049 hits inside
the skin, up to 0.17 mm. **Clip the ORIGINAL motion** (Quake 1): the remainder walked back and forth
in a 120° corner at 575 mm/s. **A skin thinner than GJK's margin is worse than none.** **The round
bottom's three numbers**, R = r + skin: curb R(1 − cos θ) 90.8 mm, overhang R sin θ 219.2, step-up
forward R(1 − sin θ) 90.8; the tallest ledge is step_height + curb, 0.39 m. **Carry by transform**;
by velocity a rider spirals out at e^(πωh) a revolution. **Steer the proxy**: teleported, a sleeping
crate is walked through and an awake one is sunk v·h/β + slop − v·h = 138 mm into. **GJK's
certificate is loose by the obstacle's size**, not its tolerance (2.3 mm off a 60 m ramp).
