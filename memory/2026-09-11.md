## The framing, which the probe decided

**The engine had no HDR bug — it was avoiding the question by construction.** Two choices held the
lid on: `k_reference_irradiance` = π (6.2, so a white surface renders at exactly 1.0) and the demo's
roughness of 0.49, which peaks at **0.8676** — just under. Change the second and the *same* shading
equation returns 11.59 at roughness 0.20 and **55,917** for a polished metal, all stored as code 255.

That framing is better than "look at all this clipping" and it came from the probe, not from a plan.

## Traps found

- **A monotonic relationship coming out backwards means the measurement is missing its target.**
  `probe_612.cpp` v1 swept the eye through a plane and found *sharper* surfaces peaking *lower* —
  the sweep never crossed the mirror direction. Same trap in the demo: a flat-faced scene at
  roughness 0.15 reports **zero** pixels over the lid. Fixed by adding a torus, curved in both
  directions, so some point satisfies the mirror condition from every viewpoint.
- **I got the Reinhard white figure wrong in a doc comment** — claimed ~768, the answer is **224**.
  Inverted the 254.5/255 threshold in the wrong space. Module 6's recurring mistake, committed in
  the module about it, caught because the harness measured rather than repeated it.
- **Figure numbers drifted out of page order again** (the resolve at 4, per-channel at 5, body
  showing the reverse). Added a **`figOrder` check to `check-page.js`** — and sweeping every lesson
  page with it found the same defect in **three published lessons**: 02-05-matrices,
  03-10-profiling-capstone, 04-01-how-gpus-work. Not fixed here (out of scope, and the fix must
  edit body fragments plus the prose's numbered references); flagged as a task.

## What was underneath — the real finding

**Neither old builder could run at all**, in two independent ways that would each have corrupted a
page had I not checked first:

1. `build_310.py` and `build_41.py` **still stamped a `STATE` block**, retired from lesson pages at
   5.7. Re-running either would have re-added 60% of a file.
2. Both read their listings from `src/` — the directory **Module 5's refactor deleted**. They had
   been unreproducible since 5.1 and nobody had noticed, because nobody had needed to rebuild them.

Fixed by retiring the STATE stamping (the amendment `build_57.py` got in 5.8) and pinning all
eleven listings from the commits that shipped each lesson (26cd723, b9bedf0). **Then proved both
rebuilt byte-identically before changing a single figure number** — which is the only thing that
makes the subsequent diff trustworthy.

**And the rendered pages turned out to be more correct than their sources.** The 2026-09-08 Module
8→9 renumber (three sites) and 4.1's `next` nav link had been applied to the shipped HTML and never
to the body fragments or listings. A rebuild would have reverted all four. Third time this drift has
been found.

## Also worth knowing
