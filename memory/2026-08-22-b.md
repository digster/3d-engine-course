## Two measurement bugs, both kept in the lesson

1. **A single distance is not a property of the format** — it depends where you fall between two
   codes. The first table went *down* between 10 m and 25 m, which is the tell.
2. **Evenly spaced probe offsets aliased against the code spacing** at 25 m (the step was ~one code
   wide). Golden-ratio offsets fixed it. Third aliasing bug in the course.

## The continuity trap, second occurrence

Changing `mesh.frag.hlsl` broke `verify_45` **again** — 4.6 gave that shader a uniform block, 4.7
gave it a sampler, and a draw with nothing bound to a sampler slot draws nothing. The standing
rule from the 4.6 session caught it.

Fixed the same way both times: **give the older harness the identity element of the new feature.**
4.6 → the camera its `static const` floats encoded; 4.7 → a 1×1 white texture, which multiplies
the tint by one. All of Lesson 4.5's pixel counts still reproduce and all six of its figure SVGs
regenerate byte for byte.

**Run every previous harness after touching a shared shader.** Twice now the breakage has been the
shader, not the C++.
